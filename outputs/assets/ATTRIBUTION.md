# Sound resources

Guitar and percussion samples: FluidR3 GM soundfont, Frank Wen and contributors, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).

Guitar MP3 source: https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/ (acoustic_guitar_steel, acoustic_guitar_nylon, electric_guitar_clean, electric_guitar_muted).

Percussion source: https://surikov.github.io/webaudiofontdata/sound/ (FluidR3 GM acoustic bass drum 35, acoustic snare 38, closed hi-hat 42).

Changes: selected MP3 notes embedded for offline playback, decoded and peak-normalized in the browser. Tight and power drum variants use different tuning, envelopes and balance from these same recorded samples. No third-party site code, analytics, branding or donation content is included.
